      How this guide is built

      Every name, description and grade is the official PlayStation 5 list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles, who agree on all 55. Each guide is written in our own words and cites its sources beside it — a specific place or step with no source earns no trust, so every guide names where its facts come from. Where guide writers are still confirming a trigger, the guide says so instead of guessing. The roadmap and the missable flag are built from the official descriptions: which story chapters and missions, which collectibles and counts, in what order. The app works fully offline and saves nothing anywhere but your own browser.

      Unofficial fan guide. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games. No Rockstar artwork or logos are used. Sources: the official trophy list on Exophase and PSNProfiles, and the trophy guides of PowerPyx, Push Square and KeenGamer.
