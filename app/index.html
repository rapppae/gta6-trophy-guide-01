      Missable trophies

      Positive Mental Attitude (bronze) is the only trophy the guides flag as possibly missable. The first meditation is Lucia’s on the balcony at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, offered after the mission Down to Earth: follow her and accept. It disappears once Chapter 1 ends, and it is unconfirmed whether it counts toward the 18 spots — do it anyway. Everything else can be finished in free-roam after the story. Stranger mission chains can have windows tied to story progress, so start each one as soon as it appears.
