      Facts that shape the plan

      • 200 hours, 4/10. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours; the story alone takes 60–80 hours.
      • One playthrough. No online trophies, no mission gold medals, no New Game+. Free-roam continues after the story, but there is no mission replay, so keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter.
      • 100% is lenient. A Long Road needs the completion checklist under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion: 300 of 397 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied. Individual trophies ask for less than the checklist does.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Leave them off for the whole run.
      • Sources. Every trophy names its sources in its guide. Names and descriptions are shown as published; the guides themselves are written in my own words.
