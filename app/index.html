Roadmap to the platinum — six stages

  1. Stage 1 — Story, Chapters 1–2. Play the story and enjoy it. Meditate at Jason's house in Chapter 1 before that spot is gone — the one missable trophy. Scan vehicles and study animals through the binoculars as you travel.
  2. Stage 2 — Story, Chapters 3–5. Finish the story, do stranger strands and side missions as they appear, and switch between Jason and Lucia for character-only content.
  3. Stage 3 — 100% completion checklist. After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion: 70 of 80 challenges, 250 stunts, every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range and drone race.
  4. Stage 4 — Strangers & Freaks. Complete the eight stranger strands and the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone.
  5. Stage 5 — Collectibles and exploration. Three card sets, 100 WAiNK spots, 100 vehicles discovered, every meditation spot, all of Leonida explored.
  6. Stage 6 — Cleanup. Partner and crime trophies, the money trophies, and whatever is left, until Vice Got a Price! pops.

Cheats disable trophies and saving, so play clean. Save by hand at the start and end of every chapter — there is no mission replay.
