Roadmap to the Platinum

Six stages, in order. Finish the story first — most missables live there — then sweep the side content, activities and collectibles before the final cleanup.

    Missable trophies are flagged MISSABLE in the list below. Do those when the moment comes: some can be retried with mission replay, others are one-time choices or events.

    Sources & how this guide was built

    Trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as published by Exophase and PSNProfiles, who agree on every name, description and grade. Each guide below is written in our own words from the official description and cites its sources beside it — a source is cited, never copied. The game had not released when this guide was written, so where a method depends on a place or step the description does not name, the guide says so and points at the description instead of inventing a location.
